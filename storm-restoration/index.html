Signs Your Roof Has Storm Damage

Check from the ground only. Never climb onto a damaged roof. If you see any of these, call us.

Missing, cracked or lifted shingles
Shingle granules collecting in your gutters
Dented gutters, downspouts or flashing
Water stains on ceilings or in the attic
Damage around chimneys, skylights or vents
Dents or cracks in siding after hail

How Your Insurance Claim Works

Most homeowners have never filed a roof claim. We walk you through every step, and we're there when the adjuster comes.

  1. Document it

    Take photos of any damage you can see safely from the ground.

  2. Free inspection

    We inspect, photograph everything and write a detailed estimate.

  3. File the claim

    Call your insurer to report the damage and open a claim.

  4. Adjuster visit

    We meet the adjuster on site so nothing gets missed.

  5. Approval

    Your insurer reviews our documentation and estimate to set the payout.

  6. Restoration

    We restore your roof, then clean up and do a final walkthrough with you.

Most claims take a few weeks from filing to payment. Coverage depends on your policy and deductible.

Storm & Insurance FAQ

Quick answers. Call us for anything else.

Does homeowners insurance cover storm damage?

Most policies cover hail, wind and snow damage, but coverage depends on your policy. Check your deductible and limits, or we can help you understand what to look for.

What should I do right after a storm?

Check for damage from the ground only if it's safe, take photos, call us for a free inspection, and contact your insurer to start a claim.

What happens if I wait too long to file?

Most policies have a filing deadline. Waiting can mean a denied claim or more expensive repairs as small damage spreads.

What does the insurance adjuster do?

The adjuster inspects your property and reviews the contractor's estimate to decide the payout. Having us there makes sure every bit of damage is documented.

Do I need multiple estimates?

Some insurers suggest it, but one detailed estimate from an experienced local storm contractor is often enough.

How long does a claim take?

Usually a few weeks from filing to payment. It can take longer after big storms or if extra documentation is needed.

Why choose a local contractor?

A local crew knows Centre County weather, building codes and insurers, responds fast after a storm, and is here afterward to stand behind the work.
